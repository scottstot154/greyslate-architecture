# GreySlate launch content

The first look uses illustrative projects and AI-generated photography. Replace these with approved project records and assets before making the site public.

| Area | Needed for launch | Helpful additions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio | Confirmed firm name, wordmark or SVG logo, short introduction, services, base city and areas served | Founder bios, headshots, studio photos, brand colors and fonts |
| Contact | Public inquiry email and person responsible for responding | Phone, office hours, public office address and social links |
| Opening image | One approved landscape photograph, ideally at least 2000 px wide, with room for the headline and a usable mobile crop | A second hero crop, caption and photo credit |
| Project records | 4–6 projects with title, category, city or region, stage, year, firm scope, short summary and longer case-study text | Area, collaborators, completion dates or timeline, and client name only if approved |
| Images | One cover and 3–8 edited images per completed project; alt text, credit and permission for each | Plans, sections or material details, labeled as drawings or renders |
| Ongoing projects | Latest named stage and at least one approved dated milestone with a short note | Approved site photo or rendering, explicitly labeled by image type |
| Renovations | At least one matched pair: same space and viewpoint before work and after completion, with descriptions and credits | More matching pairs and a note about what was retained or changed |
| Editorial ownership | Studio editor's ChatGPT sign-in email, approver and who handles photo rights | Update cadence and a backup editor |

Choose one project for the homepage. Include work that speaks to residential and commercial or institutional prospects where the portfolio supports it. Do not include private addresses, client names, plans or site images without permission.
